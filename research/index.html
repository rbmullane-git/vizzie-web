Research task files

Small data extracts used in Vizzie's usability research. Each is a census tenure table cut to ~32 areas: an area code, a name, and one measure.

  • au-melbourne-sa2-rent.csv — median weekly rent by Melbourne SA2
  • us-california-county-rent.csv — median gross rent by California county
  • uk-london-borough-renting.csv — private renting share by London borough
  • ca-ontario-cd-renting.csv — rented share by Ontario census division

Sources: Australian Bureau of Statistics, Census 2021 (CC BY 4.0) · US Census Bureau, American Community Survey (public domain) · Office for National Statistics, Census 2021 (Open Government Licence v3) · Statistics Canada, Census 2021 (Statistics Canada Open Licence). Extracts prepared by Vizzie (vizzie.org).
