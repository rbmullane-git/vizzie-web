Ready-made maps for the place you search.

Search a town, suburb or city and open Quick maps. Vizzie draws census, property and terrain layers for that place from official open sources, already joined to boundaries, so there's nothing to find, download or match up. What you get depends on what each country, state and council publishes openly, so coverage varies. This is where it stands today.

Real data you can explore right now.

Open any of these in Vizzie, no sign-up needed. Pan, filter, and edit the maps and charts to get a feel for it.

Street trees

Vancouver

150,000+ street trees by species, size, and location, mapped live in 3D.

Vehicle collisions

New York

Every recent NYC crash as a heatmap, with people injured by borough and leading causes.

Cool islands

Paris

The city's green refuges as shaded areas, by type of space and their share of tall vegetation.

Sea level rise & social vulnerability

Boston

The coastline under 9 and 36 inches of rise, over the neighbourhoods least able to absorb it.

Film locations

San Francisco

Where the movies were shot, by production company and neighbourhood.

Land use by ward

Tokyo

How the 23 special wards divide their land, shaded by the share given over to housing — a table with no geometry, joined to ward boundaries.

EV charging network

New South Wales

Every public charging site by the network that runs it, raised off the map by how many plugs it carries.

Road crashes 2023–2025

Victoria

A 200,000-row crash file streamed live, by collision type, day of the week and severity.

Population by county

United States

The 2020 census for every county — a table keyed by GEOID with no geometry, joined to county boundaries.

The cycle network

Copenhagen

Every segment of the world’s most-copied cycle network, drawn as lines by route type — and which of it is built rather than only planned.

Summer houses by municipality

Sweden

634,000 fritidshus across 290 municipalities — a national statistics database with no geometry at all, joined to municipal boundaries.

Village infrastructure by district

India

The share of villages in each district still without tap water, from the Mission Antyodaya rural survey — matched to district boundaries by name, not by code.

All of this data is already public and free. Vizzie makes it usable: connected in real time, mapped, and ready to share.

Our Mission

Public data belongs to everyone. So should the power to use it.

Make public data usable by anyone, closing the tools-and-expertise divide. Help non-specialists and mission-driven organisations put data to work. Champion open data.
