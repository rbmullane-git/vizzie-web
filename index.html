How it works

From open data to a finished map, in one place.

1

Connect

Browse and connect to thousands of open-data portals around the world, live — nothing to download or reformat.

2

Explore

Map, chart, and analyse place-based data together on one linked canvas — powerful spatial analysis, without needing to be an expert.

3

Share

Turn what you find into presentation-ready maps and interactive stories you can publish and share with a link.

What we believe

Public data belongs to everyone — so should the power to use it.

ACCESS
Make public data usable by anyone, closing the tools-and-expertise divide.
CAPACITY
Help non-specialists and mission-driven organisations actually use data.
ADVOCACY
Champion open data, interoperability, and open standards — supporting the movement.

Aligned with the Open Data Charter.
